# North Shore Process Solutions Brand Identity

## Brand Promise

Helping small businesses get their time back.

North Shore Process Solutions is a local Business Efficiency Partner. The brand should feel calm, capable, practical, and trustworthy. Technology is presented as the tool. Time saved is the product.

## Logo Concept

The primary logo uses the provided `NS` wave mark from `public/transparentLogo.png` inside a rounded white tile paired with a two-line wordmark:

- `North Shore`
- `Process Solutions`

The mark is simple enough for the website header, social avatars, proposals, invoices, and future printed materials.

## Color Palette

- Deep Navy: `#0B2545` for trust, strategy, and primary brand surfaces.
- Soft Blue: `#2F80ED` for CTAs, highlights, and progress moments.
- Cloud Background: `#F7FAFC` for calm whitespace.
- White: `#FFFFFF` for cards and clean content surfaces.
- Slate Text: `#102033` for accessible body copy.
- Muted Slate: `#5C6B7D` for supporting text.
- Border Blue Gray: `#DCE7F2` for light structure.

## Typography

The site uses Inter through `next/font` for a modern, readable, business-friendly voice.

- H1: bold, tight tracking, large scale for confidence.
- H2/H3: semibold to bold, direct and plain.
- Body: 16-20px with generous line height for easy scanning.
- Labels: small uppercase tracking for structure, used sparingly.

## Copy Voice

Write like a helpful advisor talking to a busy owner over coffee:

- Plain English over jargon.
- Practical outcomes over technical features.
- Calm confidence over hype.
- Every section should answer: “How does this save me time?”

## Button Styles

- Primary navy button for high-trust actions.
- Accent blue button for the main conversion action: `Book Your Free Process Review`.
- Outline button for secondary navigation.
- All buttons include hover, active, focus, and disabled states.

## Iconography

Lucide icons are used with rounded cards and simple line weight. Icons should support the message rather than decorate the page. Preferred themes include time, workflows, checklists, communication, people, and clarity.

## Illustration Style

Use custom UI-style illustrations instead of generic corporate stock imagery. The homepage hero visual shows the transformation from scattered manual work to organized, calmer operations.

## Spacing And Components

Spacing follows an 8px grid with generous whitespace:

- 16px for tight internal gaps.
- 24px for card padding.
- 48-80px for major section spacing.
- Rounded 16-32px cards with soft navy shadows.

Reusable components live in `components/` and shadcn-style primitives live in `components/ui/`.
